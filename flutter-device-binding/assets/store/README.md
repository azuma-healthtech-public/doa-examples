# Play Console listing assets

Generated from the azuma brand SVGs in `azuma-homepage/build/img/`, so they stay
in step with the brand rather than being redrawn by hand.

| File | Play Console field (EN / DE) | Spec |
|---|---|---|
| `app-icon-512.png` | App icon / App-Symbol | 512 × 512 PNG, opaque |
| `feature-graphic-1024x500.png` | Feature graphic / Vorstellungsgrafik | 1024 × 500 PNG, opaque |

Both are deliberately opaque: Play applies its own corner mask to the icon and
uses the feature graphic as a banner, so transparency buys nothing and renders
unpredictably. The feature graphic keeps the logo well inside the frame because
Play crops its edges on some surfaces.

The launcher icon inside the app is a separate thing, generated by
`flutter_launcher_icons` from `assets/icon/` — see the `flutter_launcher_icons`
block in `pubspec.yaml`. Changing one does not change the other.

## Regenerating

Both are rasterised with headless Chrome rather than an image editor, so the
sources stay vector and the output is reproducible:

- icon: `logo_icon_only.svg` centred on white at 512 × 512
- feature graphic: `logo.svg` at 470 px wide, with a "doa example" subtitle in
  the brand teal `#3e86a3`, centred on white at 1024 × 500

## Screenshots

`screenshots/` holds four phone captures (Screenshots für Telefon), taken on a
Galaxy S23 Ultra and post-processed:

| File | Screen |
|---|---|
| `01-entry.png` | Entry screen |
| `02-signin-methods.png` | Sign-in method picker, all four methods |
| `03-register.png` | Register, including Register with Passkey |
| `04-device-settings.png` | Device Settings |

They are **1052 × 2076**, not the phone's native 1080 × 2316, and the reason
matters if you retake them. Play caps screenshot aspect ratio at **2:1**, and a
modern phone is taller than that — 2316/1080 is 2.144:1 and gets rejected.
Trimming the status bar (100 px), the gesture bar (140 px) and a sliver off the
right edge (28 px) brings it to 1.973:1. The right-edge trim also removes the
Samsung edge-panel handle, and dropping the status bar takes the device owner's
notification icons and battery level out of a public listing, which is worth
doing regardless of the ratio.

Retaking them is `adb exec-out screencap -p > shot.png` followed by the same
crop; the numbers above are specific to this device's bar heights.
